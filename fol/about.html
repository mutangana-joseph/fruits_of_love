    About Fruits of Love Family

    Fruits of Love Family is a passionate team of over 110 members, founded in Rwanda. Our journey began in high school at CJSM (Centre de Jeunes Sourds Muets) in Huye District, Ngoma Sector — also known as Saint Gabriel School. We started small, with less than 10 members, and a big heart focused on helping deaf children from very poor families who couldn't afford basic school materials or take care of themselves.

    Our Mission

    At FRUITS OF LOVE, our mission is to contribute to the social development of Rwanda by promoting love, compassion, and hope among vulnerable communities. We are dedicated to protecting human life and improving the well-being of children, mothers, and families facing poverty, hunger, and difficult living conditions. We believe that every child deserves a healthy and brighter future, every mother deserves care and support, and every family deserves the opportunity to live with dignity and hope. Through humanitarian activities and acts of kindness, we aim to create positive and lasting change in society.

    Our Mission Includes:

    • Supporting poor mothers in hospitals through donations, encouragement, and basic humanitarian assistance.
    • Helping street children by providing care, hope, and opportunities for a better future.
    • Assisting families living in extreme poverty through outreach programs and community support.
    • Promoting unity, peace, love, and social responsibility within communities.
    • Encouraging young people and volunteers to become agents of compassion and positive transformation.
    We believe that those who understand the pain of poverty and hunger can become a source of hope to others. Together, we can lift vulnerable people from the under-margin line of life and build a society filled with humanity, dignity, and love.

    Our Vision

    Our dream is to see all street children off the streets — safe, in homes, and in school. We want to build a better future for the next generation, free from drugs and alcohol. We aim to ensure that every Rwandan child has access to safety, education, and opportunity.

    Our Activities

    • Helping Poor Children

      We support children under 18 from poor families by providing food, clothes, and hygiene items.

    • Supporting Mothers in Hospitals

      We visit hospitals to help struggling mothers with food, baby items, and sometimes hospital bills.

    • Finding Homes for Street Kids

      We rent homes for kids on the street and help them settle into a safe environment.

    • Helping with School Materials

      We provide school materials like books, pens, and uniforms for poor students.

    • Connecting People to Jobs

      We help unemployed parents and older youth find jobs to support their families.
